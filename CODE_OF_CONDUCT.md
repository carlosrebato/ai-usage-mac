# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project. Be respectful,
assume good intent, discuss ideas rather than people and protect users' private
data. Maintainers may remove abusive, discriminatory, threatening or
privacy-violating contributions and participation.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers rather than escalating them
in a public issue. A dedicated contact will be added before the repository is
opened to outside contributors.
